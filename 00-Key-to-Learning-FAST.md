# 0. Key to Learning FAST

**Short, focused sessions multiple times per day**
3 × 10 min > 1 × 30. Spaced repetition consolidates motor memory while you are away. When you first get up in the morning and right before bed are optimal.

**Use a metronome**
Start at 40–60% target tempo where you can play *without tension*. The metronome is a magnifier. Rule: play 4 bars perfectly 3× in a row → +5 BPM. Fail twice → −10 BPM. Later, clicks on 2 and 4 for swing.

**Go slow. If you can't play it perfectly, you're practicing too fast!**
Speed is a byproduct of accuracy. At slow tempo you can watch: thumb behind neck? fingers lifting too high? eyes looking *to* the next fret, not *on* this one? Perfect at 60 = clean at 120.

**Journal what you're practicing**
One line after each session, not a notebook:
`G shells A-string 80 clean, 90 rushes B-string → next 85`
Just enough to reconstruct yesterday without remembering.

**Learn patterns, not licks**
It's all about applying small, simple patterns to chord tones or the scale. Leading tones, enclosures, etc. When you learn a pattern, learn it for every note in the scale and in every hand possition.

**Make Music**
Whatever you're practicing, make music with it. Create a small loop for focused practice. Apply it to the songs in the Repertoire Lab.
