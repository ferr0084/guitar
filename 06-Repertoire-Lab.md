# 6. Repertoire Lab — 8 Tunes, One Method

All tunes in **concert keys** (as on the stand). If a tune is in `A♭` and you drilled `C` shells at `A3`, transpose the *pattern*: move the grip by semitones (`C→A♭` = −4 frets) or play in `C` for practice then in concert at the jam. Form = bar-lines, not lead-sheet engraving — bring a real book for melody.

Each tune: **Form/Key | Why | Map (CAGED/shell fret) | BH scale | Scale (solo)**.

### Warm-up — Hey Joe (kept from Ch 1)

*Not repertoire* — CAGED drill: `| C | G | D | A | E |` circle of fifths. Play once with `E-shapes` barred, once with `A-shapes`. Shows tiling before jazz harmony. Full drill in [1. CAGED](./01-CAGED.md#warm-up-tune-hey-joe).

> **How to work the tunes (all 8):**
> 1. **Shells first:** comp A-straight with `1·7·3` at 60, 2 beats per chord, in practice key (`C` shells you drilled), then concert key (−2 to −4 frets).
> 2. **BH next:** replace each `maj7→6` scale, `m7→m6` scale, `7→7` scale — play 2 beats `6`, 2 beats `°7` walking the 8-tone scale through the form.
> 3. **Solo:** stay in one box (`on 2–6` at the tune's tonic) and play `Dorian→Mixolydian→Ionian` as the changes pass — do not move hand, move *root hearing*. Then move box with CAGED to next key center (ATYA, Tune Up).
> 4. **Time:** record 5 min per tune at 70 → 90 → 110 over a week. Bring the 2 you can play *without stopping* to the jam.
